The Fio Claro case

Isa, a 35-year-old designer, and Célia, a 57-year-old seamstress, define the style of a fictional visible-mending shop. Codex images are illustrations; they do not claim real services or results.

View educational images and prompts. The final project includes a brand worksheet, three pillars, a board with six references, a map with six elements, two compared images, three applications, and a usage guide.

How to study

Read in fifteen-minute sessions and complete the practice sections. To make everything, set aside 7–11 hours, plus image-generation waits and audience conversations. No sales or automatic emotional response are promised.

You should know how to save files and use a notes, document, or presentation app. Image generation requires access to an image generator. Check your account terms. Exercises without a created file remain pending.

Progress stays in this browser. Use the journey panel to save a copy. The pages contain text, examples, and activities; they are not recorded video lessons.
